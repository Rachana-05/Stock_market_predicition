Quantum ML — ZZFeatureMap (6 Qubits)

What is ZZFeatureMap?

ZZFeatureMap encodes classical data into quantum states using Hadamard gates and ZZ interactions (two-qubit entanglement).


Circuit per layer:

H⊗n → Rz(2xᵢ) → CNOT + Rz(2(π-xᵢ)(π-xⱼ)) → repeat


Kernel: K(x₁,x₂) = |⟨φ(x₁)|φ(x₂)⟩|²


Why ZZ? The (π-xᵢ)(π-xⱼ) term captures second-order feature correlations that classical kernels cannot represent efficiently.

Problem Statement

Full-stack NSE stock market prediction using ML, DL, and QML. Predicts next-day UP/DOWN direction using a universal multi-stock model trained on 9 NSE large-caps with 45 technical features.
